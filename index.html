Use your quizzes on another device

Your quizzes are saved inside this browser, so a phone and a computer each have their own list. To move them, make a backup here, send it to yourself (Messenger, Telegram, email or Google Drive), then restore it on the other device.

Restore on this device

Restoring adds the quizzes from the backup. It never deletes what you already have here.

Formats the app understands

Mix and match as you like. The app picks the format automatically.

Question | Answer
Question [Tab] Answer   (copied from Excel or Sheets)

Answer: the answer
Q: Question text?
A: the answer
1. Question text?
a. First choice
b. Second choice
c. Third choice
Answer: B
Question 1 on one line
Answer 1 on the next line
Question 2 on one line
Answer 2 on the next line

An “Answer Key” list at the end (1. B, 2. A, …) works too. When a question has its own choices, the quiz uses them.

For identification, you can list other accepted answers with a semicolon, like Carbon dioxide; CO2. Common pairs such as CO2 and carbon dioxide, or H2O and water, and numbers like two and 2, are accepted automatically.

Explanations (optional). Add why the answer is correct. You write it, and the quiz shows it after each answer.

1. Question text?
a. First choice
b. Second choice
Answer: B
Explanation: why B is correct
(it can continue on more lines)
